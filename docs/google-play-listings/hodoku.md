## Full Description
Hodoku Revisited is the legendary HoDoKu 2.2.0 Sudoku engine, reborn for modern devices. Solve any Sudoku puzzle, generate new ones, and train your skills with the same human-style logic that made the original desktop classic famous.

Solve with 90+ human techniques across 13 families — singles, locked candidates, fish, wings, chains, and almost locked sets — with a deterministic difficulty rating on every puzzle. The 4-stage Socratic AI Coach teaches each deduction step by step: where to look, the pattern, the logic, and the conclusion. Tap "Why not D?" on any candidate and the Contradiction Prover shows exactly why that digit must fail.

Play the shared Daily Sudoku puzzle, solve the same board as players worldwide each UTC day, and share your results card. Choose Playing, Learning, or Practicing mode and work through 32 illustrated technique guides.

A tribute to Bernhard Hobiger's HoDoKu (2008–2012): every solving step is verified in lockstep against the original Java engine across 587 golden puzzles and 34,672 steps, so the difficulty rating matches the gold standard exactly.

100% free, open source, and private. No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud, no tracking. Puzzles and progress stay on your device, and everything works fully offline. Classic software, reborn by Revisited Labs.

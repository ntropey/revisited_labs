## Full Description
Logisim Revisited reborns Carl Burch's classic Logisim 2.7.1 digital logic simulator for modern devices — no Java required. Build, simulate, and learn digital electronics with the complete classic component set, rebuilt and extended with modern tooling.

Simulate circuits with a 4-state engine — 0, 1, floating Z, and error E — across multi-bit buses up to 32 bits. Drag and drop gates, plexers, arithmetic, memory, and I/O components: NOT, AND, OR, XOR, multiplexers, adders, flip-flops, registers, RAM, LEDs, and 7-segment displays.

Open standard Logisim .circ files or export your own. Verify logic with interactive 2–4 variable Karnaugh maps, Quine-McCluskey minimization, and one-click schematic synthesis straight from truth tables. Debug like a pro: click any wire or pin to trace signal propagation backward, or let the 4-stage Socratic Circuit Coach walk you through circuit debugging and K-map grouping.

Take on the Daily Logic Challenge and share your results card.

100% free, open source, and private. No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud, no tracking. Circuits stay on your device, and everything works fully offline. Classic software, reborn by Revisited Labs.

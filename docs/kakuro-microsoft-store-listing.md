## Full Description
Kakuro Revisited reborns the classic Cross Sums number puzzle, inspired by the open-source Java tools crozzsum, kakurohelper, and jkakuro. Generate puzzles with deterministic difficulty ratings, or solve with a human-style logical engine — magic blocks, cross-run intersections, and sum bounds, in the exact order a skilled player reasons.

The 4-stage Socratic AI Coach teaches every deduction without spoiling the answer, and the "Why not?" Contradiction Prover pinpoints the exact run where a wrong digit must overflow or duplicate.

Study the complete interactive Sum Combinations Cheat Sheet — every valid digit partition for every clue — and take on the shared Daily Puzzle.

100% free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later). No accounts, no tracking — boards, progress, and saves live on your device. Works offline, on any device.

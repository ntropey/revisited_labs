## Full Description
Nonogram Revisited is a modern rebirth of the classic line-logic puzzle — known as Picross and Griddlers — inspired by the beloved open-source Java games FreeNono and NonogramSolver. It pairs a deterministic human-step solving engine with a fast, offline-capable web app.

Play daily date-seeded puzzles shared worldwide, or generate your own at any size and difficulty. The 4-stage Socratic Coach teaches line logic without spoiling the solve, and the "Why not fill / Why not cross out?" Contradiction Prover demonstrates every deduction — no guessing, ever.

Every puzzle is rated deterministically by the same nine human-style techniques a good player uses.

100% free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later). No accounts, no tracking — boards and progress stay in local storage on your device. Works offline, on any device.

## Key Features
1. Solve Picross puzzles with a deterministic 9-technique human-step engine — zero guessing.
2. Play date-seeded Daily Puzzles — same board worldwide, shareable results card.
3. Learn line logic with the 4-stage Socratic AI Coach that nudges, never spoils.
4. Prove "Why not fill?" / "Why not cross out?" with the Contradiction Prover.
5. Generate puzzles at any size with deterministic difficulty ratings.
6. Play offline — no accounts, no tracking, saves stay on your device.

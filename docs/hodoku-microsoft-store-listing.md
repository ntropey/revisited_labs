## Full Description
Hodoku Revisited is the complete HoDoKu 2.2.0 Sudoku engine, rescued from Java and rebuilt as a fast, modern web app. Generate puzzles, solve with 90+ human-style techniques across 13 families — singles, fish, wings, chains, almost locked sets, and more — and watch the difficulty rater score every puzzle exactly as the original did.

Learn instead of guessing. The Socratic AI Coach walks you through any deduction in four stages — where to look, the pattern, the logic, the conclusion — and the "Why not D?" Contradiction Prover demonstrates why a wrong candidate must fail, step by step.

A tribute to Bernhard Hobiger's legendary solver: every step is verified in lockstep against the original HoDoKu 2.2.0 across 587 golden puzzles and 34,672 solving steps.

100% free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later). No accounts, no cloud, no tracking — your puzzles and progress live on your device. Works offline, on any device.

## Key Features
1. Solve any Sudoku with the full HoDoKu 2.2.0 engine — 90+ human-style techniques across 13 families.
2. Generate fresh puzzles or take on the shared Daily Puzzle with a shareable results card.
3. Learn step by step with the 4-stage Socratic AI Coach — it teaches, never spoils.
4. Prove contradictions with one click: "Why not D?" shows the exact forcing chain.
5. Train across Playing, Learning, and Practicing modes with deterministic difficulty ratings.
6. Run 100% offline — no account, no tracking, your puzzles stay on your device.
